PsyAgenda — Important Information and Terms of Use

Last updated: 6 May 2026 · Agreement version: 3

PsyAgenda is a professional tool designed to help therapists (psychologists, psychiatrists, counselors) manage appointments, client records, psychological tests, session notes, budgets, and encrypted cloud synchronization from a single application. The app is available on Android, iOS (iPhone, iPad), macOS, and Windows (Desktop) platforms.

1. Data Collection and Local Storage

PsyAgenda collects and stores the following data categories to manage therapy processes:

  • Client Information: Only first name and surname are required. All other fields — phone, email, date of birth, gender, marital status, education, profession, and therapist notes — are entirely optional.
  • Appointment/Session Data: Date, time, duration, pre-session notes, session notes, next-session notes, AI session analysis, session images, and alarm settings.
  • Psychological Test Data: Rorschach test, Projective Drawing Test, and Manual Test data.
  • Financial Data: Session fees, expenses, expense categories, and payment statuses.

All data is stored exclusively on your device's local storage (SQLite database). No data leaves your device unless cloud synchronization is enabled.

2. Data Privacy and Device Responsibility

  • Data Security and Responsibility: Data stored in PsyAgenda may contain sensitive confidential information. The security and privacy of this data are entirely your responsibility.
  • Physical Security: It is your duty to ensure the physical security of your device.
  • Digital Security: Ensuring the digital security of your device is also your responsibility.
  • Data Loss: The developer is not responsible for data loss resulting from loss, damage, theft, or software failure of the device.
  • App Lock: PsyAgenda offers an optional password lock feature. If you forget both your app lock password and your security question answer, you will permanently lose access to your data.

3. Cloud Synchronization (Google Drive)

  • PsyAgenda optionally allows you to synchronize your data across devices via Google Drive. This feature is available on all platforms and is disabled by default.
  • Google Drive: The application only requests the "drive.appDataFolder" scope (app-private, user-invisible storage).
  • End-to-End Encryption: All data uploaded to cloud services is encrypted client-side with your synchronization password (PBKDF2 + AES-256-GCM).
  • Sync Password: If you lose this password, you will permanently lose access to your encrypted data on the cloud.

4. AI Usage and Data Sharing

  • PsyAgenda uses the Google Gemini API for various clinical analysis features. AI features are optional.
  • Data Transmitted: During AI analysis, session notes, client age and gender, test responses, and images are sent to the Google Gemini API.
  • API Key: You use your own Google Gemini API key. This key is stored encrypted on your device.
  • Anonymity Responsibility: It is entirely your responsibility not to enter any personal information that could reveal your client's identity into free-text fields.
  • Google Data Policy: Data sent to the Google Gemini API is subject to Google's terms of service. Free API tier: Google may use the data sent to improve its AI models. Paid API tier: Google does not use data sent on the paid plan for model training. Given the sensitivity of clinical data, the paid tier is recommended.
  • Clinical Responsibility: All evaluations produced by AI are solely supportive tools and preliminary analyses. All clinical responsibility rests with the user.

5. Backup and Data Recovery

  • You can export your data as an encrypted backup file (.thnbak).
  • Backup files are encrypted using AES-256-GCM. If you lose this password, you will not be able to restore your backup file.
  • Regular backups are entirely your responsibility.

6. Notifications and Communication

  • Session reminder and alarm notifications operate locally on your device.
  • The application optionally helps you send appointment notifications to your clients via SMS, WhatsApp, or email. Messages are not sent directly by the app; instead, the app opens your device's SMS/WhatsApp/email application with a pre-filled message, and delivery occurs only with your manual confirmation.

7. Device Permissions

PsyAgenda may request device permissions to provide certain features (camera, notifications, contacts, etc.). All permissions are optional, are only requested when the related feature is used, and can be denied. Denying a permission only disables the related feature. A detailed description of requested permissions is provided in the Privacy Policy.

8. License and Subscription

  • Trial Period: The application offers a 30-day free trial from the initial installation.
  • Expiration: When the trial period or subscription expires, the application switches to read-only mode.
  • Subscription Types: Monthly (1 month) and annual (1 year) subscription options are available. All purchases are processed through Apple App Store or Google Play Store; your payment information is handled solely by the respective platform, and PsyAgenda does not access your payment details. Additionally, a Lifetime license can only be granted by the developer via gift code; there is no direct purchase option.
  • Auto-Renewal: Your subscription automatically renews unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. The renewal fee is automatically charged to your Apple ID or Google Play account after the current period ends. The renewed period is billed at the current price at the time of purchase.
  • Cancelling Your Subscription: You can cancel your active subscription at any time. On iOS devices: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. On Android devices: Google Play Store → Subscriptions. Cancellation must be made at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; otherwise the subscription will renew for one more period.
  • Pricing and Duration: Current subscription prices and durations are displayed on the License page within the application and on the store purchase screen. Apple and Google may apply regional pricing variations; the price shown on the store purchase screen is the one that applies.
  • Billing and Refunds: Subscription fees are charged at the end of the free trial period (if any), or at the time of purchase if no trial applies. Refund requests are subject to the refund policies of the Apple App Store / Google Play; PsyAgenda cannot directly intervene in the refund process. You must contact the respective store's support channel for refunds.

9. Reports and Exports

PsyAgenda offers reports and data exports in DOCX (Word), PDF, and XLSX (Excel) formats. Exported files are not encrypted and are unprotected. Securing these files is your responsibility.

10. Third-Party Services

  • Google Gemini API: For AI analysis features (optional).
  • Google Drive API: For cloud synchronization (optional).
  • Google Play Store / Apple App Store: For subscription purchases.
  • RevenueCat: An anonymous per-device user identifier (App User ID) and the store purchase receipt are shared for subscription purchase flow and receipt validation. No personal data (name, email, session notes, confidential client information) is shared.

Outside the user-controlled flows explicitly listed above (synchronization, AI, support emails), the developer has no access to any user data.

13. Account and Data Deletion

  • Account Structure: PsyAgenda does not require an in-app account to be created. Google sign-in is used only for optional synchronization and operates through the user's own Google account. Therefore, no separate "account deletion" process is needed; data deletion is directly under user control.
  • Deleting Local Data: Via Settings > Storage > Danger Zone > "Delete All Local Data", all data on this device (clients, appointments, session notes, tests, financial records, attached files, settings, passwords) is permanently deleted. The action requires typed confirmation (DELETE ALL DATA) and cannot be undone.
  • Deleting Drive Data: Via Settings > Sync > Danger Zone > "Delete All Drive Data", encrypted backups, images, and the manifest on Google Drive are permanently deleted; this device's sync connection is disconnected. Local data is preserved. Other active devices on the same account may re-upload their own data on the next sync; to keep Drive permanently clean across all devices, you must repeat this action on each device.
  • Uninstalling the App: On iOS, Settings > General > iPhone Storage > PsyAgenda > Delete App; on Android, Settings > Apps > PsyAgenda > Storage > Clear Data / Uninstall removes all local data. On macOS/Windows, uninstalling the app removes the app-specific local database files.
  • Backups Not Auto-Deleted: Encrypted .thnbak backups exported voluntarily by the user remain on the user's device or external media; managing these backups is the user's responsibility.
