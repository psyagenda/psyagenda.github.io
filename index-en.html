PsyAgenda

Smart agenda for therapists.

A smart agenda application that enables therapists to manage their sessions, clients, tests, and financial processes from one place.

Calendar & Appointment Management

Plan your appointments easily with daily, weekly, and monthly calendar views. Recurring sessions and drag-and-drop support.

Client Management

Create detailed cards for each client. Session history, notes, tests, and financial information gathered in one place.

AI-Powered Analysis

Session analysis with Google Gemini API, Rorschach and drawing test evaluation, comprehensive AI client reports.

Test Modules

Administer Rorschach, projective drawing, and manual tests digitally. Download reports as Word/PDF.

Budget & Financial Tracking

Session fees, income-expense tracking, overdue debt alerts, and financial analysis charts.

Encrypted Synchronization

AES-256 encrypted sync across devices via Google Drive. No plaintext stored on the server.

Backup & Restore

Back up your data as an encrypted .thnbak file; restore anytime.

100% Local Data Storage

All your data is stored only on your device. Synchronization and AI are entirely optional.

Platforms

PsyAgenda is available on Android, iOS (iPhone, iPad), macOS, and Windows (desktop).
